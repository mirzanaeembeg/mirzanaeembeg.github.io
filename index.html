Thesis06Memory

BanglaGuard

A multimodal deep learning framework for audio-visual detection of inappropriate content in Bangla children's cartoon videos. It combines EfficientNet-based CNNs for visual features, a BiLSTM for temporal modelling, and a spectrogram-based CNN for Bangla audio classification.

87.02%accuracy on a purpose-built dataset of 871 clips
video frames→EfficientNet→BiLSTM
Bangla audio→spectrogram→CNN
fused → appropriate / inappropriate
