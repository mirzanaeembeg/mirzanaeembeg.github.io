Thesis · In Progress
BanglaGuard: A Multimodal Deep Learning Framework for Audio-Visual Detection of Inappropriate Content in Bangla Children's Cartoon Videos

Overview: Combines EfficientNet CNNs (visual), BiLSTM (temporal), and Whisper (Bangla speech) to flag unsafe video segments — currently 72.6% accuracy on 339 clips, with dataset expansion ongoing.

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Shahriar Mahbub

Course Paper · CSE4138
Digital Preservation of Culinary Heritage: A System for Classifying Bangladeshi Desserts and Generating Traditional Recipes

Overview: A deep learning pipeline that classifies 4,118 Bangladeshi dessert images across 27 categories (EfficientNet-B0 vs ResNet-18) and generates traditional recipes from a corpus of 81 authentic recipes (GPT-2 vs T5, with GPT-2 performing best).

Contributions:

  • Multi-modal dataset of dessert images and authentic recipes — publicly available on Mendeley.
  • CNN comparison for dessert classification and LLM-based recipe generation.
  • A framework for digital preservation of culinary heritage.
