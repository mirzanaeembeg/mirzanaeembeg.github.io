CSE4138 Soft Computing Lab Term Assignment

This assignment paper is titled "Digital Preservation of Culinary Heritage: A System for Classifying Bangladeshi Desserts and Generating Traditional Recipes."

Abstract: The paper presents a deep learning pipeline for preserving Bangladeshi dessert heritage by classifying 4,118 dessert images across 27 categories and generating recipes from a parallel corpus of 81 authentic traditional recipes.

Approach: It uses EfficientNet-B0 and ResNet-18 for dessert image classification, and GPT-2 plus T5 for recipe generation, highlighting GPT-2’s superior performance with a BLEU score of 0.0129 and a perplexity of 1.01.

Dataset: A curated dessert dataset of 4,118 images across 27 classes and 81 recipe entries supports both classification and generation. The dataset is publicly available at here.

Contributions:

  • Multi-modal dataset combining dessert images and authentic recipes.
  • Detailed comparison of EfficientNet-B0 and ResNet-18 for Bangladeshi dessert classification.
  • Use of GPT-2 and T5 for generating culturally relevant recipes from dessert class input.
  • Framework for digital preservation of culinary heritage through machine learning.
  • Build a recipe text dataset in excel-sheet for future culinary informatics research.
